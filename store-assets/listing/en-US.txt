=== APP NAME (max 30) ===
WBW · เดินรอบดอย

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80) ===
Your trail map, group chat and emergency button for Walk Beyond the Wild.

=== FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000) ===
Walk Beyond the Wild — เดินรอบดอย — is Mae Fah Luang University Student Union's
walking event. This is the app participants carry on the day.

THE TRAIL
See the whole route and every base on one map, with your own position on it.
Tap a base to find out what happens there, how far away it is, and how many
people have reached it already. Start a walk and the route fills in behind you
as you go, with your distance, pace and the next base ahead.

YOUR GROUP
Every participant belongs to a group. The group has its own chat — for finding
each other, saying where you are, and asking the staff walking with you.

HELP, QUICKLY
Hold SOS for three seconds. The call goes out immediately, without waiting for a
GPS fix, and your position follows the moment the phone has one. Your group's
staff are asked first; if nobody answers, it escalates to the whole event. While
you wait, the screen shows who is coming for you, along with your blood type and
emergency contact for whoever arrives first — read from the phone, so it is still
there on a ridge with no signal.

YOUR PASS
A QR code staff scan to check you in at each base, with your bib number, group
and trail stamps. It works offline.

SAY WHAT IT WAS LIKE
When staff scan you in at a base, the app asks what that place was like — the
view, and the space itself. At the end of the route it asks about the day's
activities. The organisers read the answers.

YOUR AVATAR
Pick a mark for yourself in Settings — a pine, a deer, a butterfly. They are
drawn in the same halftone as the flower on your home screen, and yours appears
beside your messages and next to your name in the group.

WHAT IT NEEDS
Location, to draw your position on the trail and to send it with an emergency
call. Camera, only for staff scanning passes. Both can be refused and the rest of
the app still works.

Built by the MFU Student Union.
