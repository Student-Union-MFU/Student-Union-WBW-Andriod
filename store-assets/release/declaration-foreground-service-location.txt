FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION — Play Console declaration
Task category: Background location updates → Other
Video: store-assets/release/fgs-location-demo.mp4

WHAT TO PUT IN THE "OTHER" DESCRIPTION FIELD
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Recording a walk the user has started: distance travelled and progress along a
fixed 8 km event route, shown only to that user, while their screen is off and
the phone is in their pocket.

WHY NOT THE OTHER CATEGORIES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not "User-initiated location sharing": the service transmits nothing. Every
fix it receives is folded into an in-memory distance total and published to the
app's own screen. No location from this service reaches our servers or any
third party.

Not "Navigation": the app gives no directions. It draws the walker's position
on a route that is fixed before the event and counts down the metres to the
next checkpoint. It never tells anyone which way to go.

Not "Geofencing": the app registers no geofences and takes no action on
entering or leaving an area. Checkpoint check-ins are performed by a staff
member scanning the participant's pass, not by their location.

WHY THE PERMISSION IS NEEDED AT ALL
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Walk Beyond the Wild is a one-day charity trail walk. Participants walk about
8 km between checkpoints on the university grounds, which takes some hours,
with the phone pocketed for nearly all of it. Location tracking scoped to a
visible screen would stop counting the moment the screen slept and then present
a total that silently omitted most of the walk — a wrong number shown
confidently. The tracking has to outlive the UI or it is not worth having.

HOW IT BEHAVES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- It starts only when the participant taps START WALKING. It never starts on
  its own, at boot, or on launch.
- While it runs it posts an ongoing, non-dismissible notification titled
  "Recording your walk", showing the distance so far and carrying a Stop
  action that ends the service from the notification itself.
- It stops on STOP, on the notification's Stop action, or when the process
  ends. It is START_NOT_STICKY: the system does not restart it, because a
  restarted service would come back as a fresh session claiming to be the same
  walk.
- The app requests no background location permission. Location is granted as
  "While using the app"; the foreground service is what makes the walk
  continue to be recorded from there.

WHAT THE VIDEO SHOWS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
A single unedited screen recording, in order: the trail map; the location
permission granted as "While using the app"; START WALKING tapped by the user;
the walk running with distance climbing; the ongoing notification in the
shade; the app sent to the background with the notification still counting up;
the app reopened with the distance accrued while it was away; STOP tapped; the
notification gone.
