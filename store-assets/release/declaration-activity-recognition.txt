WBW is the participant app for Walk Beyond the Wild, a one-day charity trail
walk run by the Student Union of Mae Fah Luang University. Participants walk a
fixed route of about 8 km between checkpoints on the university grounds.

The app requests android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION for one purpose: to
read the device's hardware step counter (Sensor.TYPE_STEP_COUNTER) while the
participant has a walk in progress, so the app can show them their step count
next to distance and speed on their own walk screen.

How the data is used:

- It is read only after the user has explicitly started a walk in the app, and
  only for the duration of that walk. Nothing is read when no walk is running.
- It is displayed only to that user, on their own device.
- It is not transmitted off the device. It is not sent to our servers, not
  stored in any backend, not shared with any third party, and not used for
  advertising, analytics, profiling or any other secondary purpose. The count
  is held in memory by the walk service and discarded when the walk ends.
- The permission is optional. If the user denies it, or the device has no
  step-counting sensor, the app continues to work normally and shows the step
  count as unavailable; walk distance is measured from location instead.
- The data is not used for any health, medical, fitness-coaching, insurance,
  employment or credit-related purpose. The app does not integrate with Health
  Connect and requests no health data permissions beyond this one.
